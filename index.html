Who I am and what I can do

Who I am

I’m an Innovative Developer, Problem Solver & Visionary Builder who enjoys turning ideas into practical digital solutions. I’m constantly learning, experimenting, and looking for better ways to solve problems with technology. I care about more than just writing code — I want to understand the problem, think through the solution, and build something that is useful, reliable, and easy to use.

What I can do

I can turn ideas, designs, and requirements into functional web applications. I build responsive and user-friendly interfaces with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and TypeScript, and I can bring designs from Figma to life with clean, structured code. On the backend, I work with JavaScript, TypeScript, and Python to build application logic, APIs, authentication systems, and data-driven features. I’m comfortable working with databases such as PostgreSQL, MySQL, and MongoDB, as well as Firebase for applications that need a flexible backend and real-time capabilities. I also use Git and GitHub to manage projects, collaborate, track changes, and maintain a clean development workflow.

Why hire me

Because I’m a builder. Give me an idea, a problem, or even something that doesn't quite make sense yet, and I’ll work to understand it, break it down, and turn it into something practical. I’m constantly learning, experimenting, and pushing myself beyond what I already know. When I encounter something unfamiliar, I don't see it as a reason to stop — I see it as something to figure out. I bring curiosity to the problem, persistence to the process, and ownership to what I build.
